<Section n="05" title="What it guarantees">

<Guarantees>
  <Guarantee story="ItOpensAndSelects">Arrow keys walk the entries, Enter selects, and typing jumps by first letter.</Guarantee>
  <Guarantee story="EscapeReturnsFocus">Escape closes it and focus goes back to the trigger, after the exit transition rather than during it.</Guarantee>
  <Guarantee story="DisabledSaysWhy">A disabled entry carries its reason, and the reason is reachable.</Guarantee>
  <Guarantee>The trigger stays sunk while the panel is open, and its chevron turns over.</Guarantee>
</Guarantees>
