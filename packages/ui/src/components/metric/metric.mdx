<Section

  <div className="w-64">
    <Metric

</Section>

<Section
  n="02"
  title="Polarity is required, and that is the point"
  intro="Errors going up and crash-free sessions going up are the same arrow and opposite news. A component that guessed would be right half the time and green while the product burns the other half."
>

<Matrix
  columns={['rose', 'fell']}
  rows={['up-is-bad', 'up-is-good', 'neutral']}
  cell={(direction, polarity) => (
    <Metric
      comparison={compareMetric(
        direction === 'rose' ? 130 : 70,
        100,
        polarity,
      )}
      label={polarity}
      value={direction === 'rose' ? '130' : '70'}
    />
  )}
/>

<P>
  Read down the second column. The arrow is the same in every row and the colour
  is not, because the arrow reports the movement and the colour reports the
  verdict.
</P>

</Section>

<Section
  n="03"
  title="Nothing to compare against is not zero per cent"
  intro="compareMetric returns nothing in two cases, and they are different cases: an all-time figure has no window before it, and a previous window of zero makes the change undefined rather than infinite."
>

<P>
  Naive arithmetic prints <code>+100 %</code> for both. Neither is true, and a
  headline reading <code>+100 %</code> on the morning a project received its
  first event is the kind of number that gets escalated.
</P>

</Section>

<Section n="04" title="Guidelines">

<DoDont>
  <Do
    example={
      <div className="w-56">
        <Metric
          comparison={compareMetric(41, 63, 'up-is-bad')}
          comparisonLabel="vs previous"
          label="New issues"
          value="41"
        />
      </div>
    }
  >
    Say what the comparison is against.
  </Do>
  <Dont
    example={
      <div className="w-56">
        <Metric
          comparison={{ percent: -35, tone: 'negative' }}
          label="New issues"
          value="41"
        />
      </div>
    }
  >
    A bare percentage. Against what, over how long, nobody can tell.
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do
    example={
      <div className="w-56">
        <Metric
          caption="Nothing to compare against"
          label="Events"
          value="900"
        />
      </div>
    }
  >
    Say there is no comparison.
  </Do>
  <Dont
    example={
      <div className="w-56">
        <Metric
          comparison={{ percent: 100, tone: 'negative' }}
          label="Events"
          value="900"
        />
      </div>
    }
  >
    Invent one. A previous window of zero is not a hundred per cent rise.
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

</Section>

<Section n="06" title="What it guarantees">

<Guarantees>
  <Guarantee story="States">The arrow follows the movement and the colour follows the polarity, independently.</Guarantee>
  <Guarantee story="NothingToCompare">A missing or zero previous window draws no change at all.</Guarantee>
  <Guarantee story="Unchanged">A change of exactly zero draws no arrow.</Guarantee>
  <Guarantee story="AsALink">With <code>render</code> the whole tile is one link, not a figure with a link inside it.</Guarantee>
</Guarantees>
